zSMTH Child Safety Standards

Identity and scope

“zSMTH水木社区(水木清华BBS)客户端” (com.zfdang.zsmth_android) is an independently developed third-party client by zfdang. It is not the official NewSMTH app and does not represent the forum operator. These standards apply to accessing, publishing and sharing content through this client. Forum accounts, posts, private messages and server content are managed by the forum operator.

Prohibited content and conduct

zSMTH prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including CSAM, grooming, sexualization of minors, sexual extortion and soliciting or distributing such material. Being a third-party client or not targeting children does not exempt the client from child safety responsibilities.

In-app reporting

Open the main menu, choose Child Safety and Reporting, then Report to Forum Administrator. Compose a private message in this app, then sign in to NewSMTH to send it to forum administrator sysop inside this app, without opening a browser or email app. Review the report before sending and check sent messages after success. Errors are displayed if submission fails. Reports are received through the NewSMTH forum system and handled by the forum operator. Standards and client contact details are available without signing in.

Include the post URL, board, author ID, time and concern. Do not download, forward or attach illegal images or videos, or provide unnecessary personal information. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Handling and responsibilities

The forum operator reviews forum reports and controls server-side removal and account moderation. The client developer cannot directly delete server posts or ban forum accounts and does not promise decisions or response times on behalf of the forum operator. When notified of child safety concerns, the developer reviews client-related issues and takes appropriate action within the client features under their control, referring forum content concerns to forum moderation. Suspected CSAM is reported to competent regional or national authorities as required by applicable law, and the developer cooperates with lawful investigations. The client complies with applicable child safety laws.

Client child safety contact

Designated contact: zfdang, me@zfdang.com, for client child safety concerns and Google Play compliance communications. Forum content reports go to sysop through in-app private messages. See the privacy policy.
